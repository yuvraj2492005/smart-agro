Common Crop Diseases

🍅 Tomato Leaf Disease

Symptoms: Yellow spots, leaf damage.

Treatment: Use fungicide and remove infected leaves.

🌾 Rice Blast Disease

Symptoms: Brown spots on leaves.

Treatment: Use recommended fungicides and proper irrigation.

🥔 Potato Blight

Symptoms: Dark patches on leaves.

Treatment: Use disease-resistant seeds and sprays.

🌱 Cotton Leaf Disease

Symptoms: Leaf curling and spots.

Treatment: Use pest control and balanced fertilizer.
